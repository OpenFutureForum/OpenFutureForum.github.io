Publishing principles

Open by design

These projects are built to be inspected, challenged and improved.

Traceable

Sources, methods and limitations are published alongside substantive findings.

Practical

Research is organized around decisions executives and community leaders actually face.

Correctable

Public repositories create a visible path for review, corrections and future releases.
